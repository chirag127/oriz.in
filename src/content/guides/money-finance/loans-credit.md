Credit is useful when it is priced, sized, and timed deliberately. The same facility becomes a trap when the minimum payment becomes the plan, when the tenure is chosen only to lower the EMI, or when the loan is used to preserve a lifestyle the income cannot sustain.

This guide covers the four credit decisions most Indian households actually face: a home loan, a personal or gold loan, a credit card, and a consumer instalment product. It also covers the credit report that decides the price of all four.

## The decision order

1. **Protect the monthly cash flow.** List essential outflows, existing EMIs, insurance premiums and the emergency reserve contribution.
2. **Compare the total cost, not the EMI.** A longer tenure lowers the payment and raises total interest. A processing fee is part of the cost even when it is deducted from the disbursal.
3. **Check the worst case.** What happens if the rate resets, the income stops, or the gold price falls? A loan that only works in the base case is not affordable.
4. **Protect liquidity.** Do not use the emergency reserve to make a prepayment or a down payment. The reserve is what prevents the next surprise from becoming a new loan.
5. **Read the credit report before applying.** Errors, duplicate accounts and stale enquiries are cheaper to fix before a lender prices the risk.

## Home loans

A home loan is usually the largest and longest credit decision. The EMI is fixed for a period, but the split changes: early payments are mostly interest. The [home-loan EMI guide](/money-finance/loans-credit/home-loan-emi-breakup-principal-interest/) works through the ₹50 lakh example and the amortisation curve. The [prepayment guide](/money-finance/loans-credit/prepay-home-loan-or-invest-the-surplus/) compares extra payments with investing and prices the liquidity trade-off.

Three practical checks:

- confirm whether the rate is fixed or floating, and how the reset works;
- ask for the amortisation schedule at disbursement and verify the rate and tenure;
- decide whether a prepayment should reduce the tenure or the EMI, and confirm it in writing.

The [EMI calculator](/tools/loan-emi/) and [amortisation calculator](/tools/loan-amortization/) show the arithmetic. A lender's quote is still the final source for fees and reset terms.

## Personal loans, gold loans, and consumer credit

Unsecured personal loans are priced for risk. A gold loan is secured by pledged metal, which can make it cheaper, but the jewellery is at risk if repayment slips. The [personal loan versus gold loan comparison](/money-finance/loans-credit/personal-loan-vs-gold-loan-cost/) prices both. The [credit-card bill-cycle guide](/money-finance/loans-credit/credit-card-bill-cycle-math-minimum-due-trap/) explains why the minimum due is a financing decision, not a payment plan. The [BNPL guide](/money-finance/loans-credit/buy-now-pay-later-bnpl-fine-print/) reads the fine print that turns an instalment into a high-cost loan.

Before taking any of them, ask:

- What is the effective annual cost including fees?
- What is the consequence of a missed payment?
- Does the product create a claim on an asset or only on future income?
- Is the underlying purchase necessary, or is the loan making it feel affordable?

## Credit reports and CIBIL

A credit score is a summary of repayment behaviour, utilisation, credit mix, enquiries and account age. [CIBIL score mechanics](/money-finance/loans-credit/cibil-score-mechanics-factors/) explains the weights; the [free annual report guide](/money-finance/loans-credit/cibil-free-annual-report/) covers the dispute route. The [score-at-22 guide](/money-finance/loans-credit/credit-score-build-at-22-cibil-mechanics/) is the early-career version.

Check the report at least once a year, before a major application. Dispute factual errors in writing and keep the acknowledgement. Do not apply to several lenders in a short window just to compare offers; each hard enquiry can leave a footprint.

## What this category will not do

It will not tell you that debt is always bad or that a particular lender is best. It will show the arithmetic, the clauses and the trade-offs so that the decision is yours. If a payment does not leave room for food, insurance, the emergency reserve and the existing obligations, the loan is too large regardless of the approval letter.

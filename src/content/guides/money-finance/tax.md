Tax is a record-keeping problem before it is a rate problem. The taxpayer who has the Form 16, the AIS, the capital-gains statement and the evidence for every deduction can answer a notice. The taxpayer who reconstructs the year in July is guessing.

This guide is a map of the Indian income-tax decisions that affect salaried people, investors, freelancers and NRIs. It does not replace the Income-tax Act, the current Finance Act, the portal instructions or a qualified professional. Rates and thresholds change; the workflow below is the durable part.

## Start with the records

Before choosing a regime or an investment, collect:

- Form 16 from each employer;
- the AIS and Form 26AS;
- bank, dividend and interest statements;
- capital-gains statements from brokers and fund houses;
- rent receipts, home-loan interest certificates and insurance premium proofs;
- business or freelance books, invoices and GST returns where applicable.

The [26AS and AIS guide](/money-finance/tax/form-26as-and-ais-reconciliation/) explains how to reconcile the taxman's view with your own. The [income-tax portal](https://www.incometax.gov.in/) is the source for filing and responses.

## Choose the regime with your numbers

The old and new regimes are not a permanent binary answer. The old regime may reward deductions for housing, insurance, retirement and education; the new regime may win when those deductions are small. The [old versus new regime walkthrough](/money-finance/tax/old-vs-new-regime-calculator-walkthrough/) runs the arithmetic with actual numbers. Use the current year's slabs and your own eligibility.

Do not buy an insurance policy or a long-lock investment only to create a deduction. The product must make sense without the tax benefit. A deduction reduces the tax on a rupee; it does not make a bad rupee good.

## Salary, TDS and advance tax

Employers deduct TDS based on the declarations you submit. A wrong declaration can produce a refund later or a demand with interest. The [TDS sections guide](/money-finance/tax/tds-sections-salaried-must-know/) covers the common salary deductions. The [advance-tax guide](/money-finance/tax/advance-tax-instalment-schedule/) explains the instalment schedule and the interest rules for income that is not fully covered by TDS.

If you change jobs, have more than one employer, or receive a bonus or vested equity, check the total tax position rather than assuming the new payroll has the full picture.

## Capital gains

Capital gains are not one tax. The holding period and the asset class decide the rate. The [capital-gains guide](/money-finance/tax/capital-gains-fy-2025-26-rates/) covers the rate card and the traps between long-term and short-term treatment. Before selling property, equity, gold or debt, identify:

- the acquisition date and cost;
- the sale date and consideration;
- the applicable holding period;
- the exemptions or deductions you may claim;
- the reporting schedule in the return.

The tax on a gain can change the decision to sell. Model it before the transaction, not after.

## Notices and corrections

A notice is a request for information or a proposed adjustment, not automatically a demand to pay. The [tax-notice guide](/money-finance/tax/got-a-tax-notice-next-steps/) explains the common sections and the first 30 days. Read the deadline, match the notice to the return, and respond through the official channel. Keep the acknowledgement.

If a mistake is yours, correct it with the appropriate updated return or response. If the notice is wrong, respond with evidence. Ignoring either one is the expensive option.

## Freelancers, side income and GST

Freelance income brings presumptive taxation, advance tax and possibly GST. The [freelance tax guide](/business/small-business/freelance-income-tax-44ada-presumptive-gst/) covers the 44ADA framework and the audit lines. The [GST registration guide](/business/small-business/gst-registration-side-income-threshold/) explains the threshold and the cases where registration may be required earlier.

Do not treat a marketplace's TCS or a client's TDS as the final tax. Both are credits; the annual return reconciles them.

## The working rule

File on time, reconcile the records, keep the evidence, and verify the current rules before acting. The goal is to pay the tax the law requires, not to find a shortcut that survives until the first notice. Start with [the regime walkthrough](/money-finance/tax/old-vs-new-regime-calculator-walkthrough/), then read [capital gains](/money-finance/tax/capital-gains-fy-2025-26-rates/) and [26AS/AIS](/money-finance/tax/form-26as-and-ais-reconciliation/).

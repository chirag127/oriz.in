Every financial task in India has an official portal, and almost every portal has three fake lookalikes
ranking above it in search results. The bookmark list below is the difference between a two-minute task and a
₹40,000 fraud, and between a claim processed and a claim stuck for months. Organised by the job you are
trying to do, with the lookalike problem named where it matters. Bookmark these once; the list changes
slower than any app store.

## Money that is yours: retirement and savings

| Portal               | URL                                          | What you do there                              |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| EPFO member portal   | unifiedportal-mem.epfindia.gov.in            | UAN passbook, withdrawal, transfer, nomination |
| NPS (CRRA)           | npstrust.org.in / cra-nsdl.com               | Statement of Transaction, tier control, exit   |
| PPF/SSA accounts     | your bank's portal (India Post Payments too) | Passbook, loan against PPF, extension          |
| Atal Pension         | npstrust.org.in                              | APY statement, auto-debit status               |
| Sovereign Gold Bonds | your demat/RBI Retail Direct                 | Holdings, interest dates, early-exit window    |
| RBI Retail Direct    | rbiretaildirect.org.in                       | T-Bills, SGBs, G-secs primary bids             |

The EPFO passbook is the most under-checked document in Indian personal finance: employees discover missing
deposits (employer deducted but did not remit) years late, when the correction window has become a dispute.
Check it annually against your salary slip's EPF line. UAN activation and KYC live on the same unified
portal; the EPFO's main site is [epfindia.gov.in](https://www.epfindia.gov.in).

## Tax: where the year is won or lost

| Portal              | URL              | What you do there                                      |
| ------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Income-tax e-filing | incometax.gov.in | ITR filing, Form 26AS, AIS/TIS, e-proceedings, notices |
| TRACES              | traces.gov.in    | Form 16/16A downloads, TDS status                      |
| GST (if you file)   | gst.gov.in       | Returns, GSTR-2B reconciliation                        |
| MCA master data     | mca.gov.in       | Verify any company's existence, charges, directors     |

The e-filing portal is also the notice box: every communication — intimation, defect notice, refund notice —
lands in the e-proceedings tab, and ignoring it is how ₹2,000 mismatches become ₹20,000 demands. The AIS
(Annual Information Statement) reconciliation before each filing season is the single highest-value hour in
the tax calendar. The portal is [incometax.gov.in](https://incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/).

## Identity and documents: the layer everything else sits on

| Portal             | URL                     | What you do there                                                 |
| ------------------ | ----------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| DigiLocker         | digilocker.gov.in       | Issued documents: PAN, Aadhaar, vehicle RC, insurance, marksheets |
| UIDAI              | myaadhaar.uidai.gov.in  | Address update, Aadhaar-PAN linking status                        |
| NSDL / Protean PAN | protean-tinpan.nsdl.com | PAN application, correction, Aadhaar-link status                  |
| mParivahan / Vahan | vahan.parivahan.gov.in  | RC, insurance validity, transfer of ownership                     |

DigiLocker's issued documents are legally equivalent to physical ones under the IT Rules — the traffic
policeman accepting your DigiLocker RC is the rule working, not a courtesy.

## Protection: the portals that get your money back

| Portal                     | URL                      | What you do there                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Cyber-crime reporting      | cybercrime.gov.in        | Fraud complaints within the golden hour, financial fraud hold |
| RBI Integrated Ombudsman   | cms.rbi.org.in           | Bank/NBFC complaints after 30 days unanswered                 |
| SEBI SCORES                | scores.sebi.gov.in       | Market-intermediary complaints                                |
| IRDAI Bima Bharosa         | bimabharosa.irdai.gov.in | Insurance complaints, grievance history                       |
| National Consumer Helpline | consumerhelpline.gov.in  | e-Daakhil consumer-court filing                               |

The cyber-crime portal's financial-fraud reporting is time-critical: the money that is still in the banking
chain in the first hours is the money that gets frozen. Every household should have one member who knows
that portal's complaint flow cold.

## The lookalike problem, stated precisely

Search engines monetise finance-intent queries, so every official portal above has sponsored clones: EPFO
helpdesk sites charging ₹1,999 for a free withdrawal, CIBIL improvement services selling what the bureau
gives free, "ITR helpline" numbers that harvest OTPs. The pattern that identifies a real portal: the domain
is a government second-level domain (.gov.in, .nic.in) or the regulated entity's own domain (cibil.com,
gst.gov.in, incometax.gov.in). Sponsored results for "EPFO withdrawal" are the highest-conversion fraud
surface in Indian finance right now — the 2024-2025 cyber-cell advisories repeatedly flag them. Never
authenticate on a page you reached through a sponsored link.

## Two everyday jobs the list quietly solves

**The FASTag and challan tangle.** Vehicle money leaks through unclear challans and dead FASTag wallets. The
Parivahan family of services and the NHAI's IHMCL pages (ihmcl.co.in, the tag-issuer list) settle
double-charge disputes in minutes; most people pay twice instead because they never reach the official page
and call the number a sponsored result showed them.

**The scholarship and subsidy claim.** The National Scholarship Portal, the state scholarship boards and the
DBT status check (dbtbharat.gov.in) are where a family's ₹10,000-₹50,000 of annual entitlement either arrives
or dies of a stuck application. The DBT status page shows which step of which scheme is holding the payment —
information no agent will sell you honestly, because the fix is free.

## Building the bookmark folder that survives

1. **One folder, synced, named "gov-fin".** Phone and laptop, same folder, so the phone can file the fraud
   complaint the laptop started.
2. **Logins once, properly.** Each portal gets a password-manager entry at setup; the portals that matter
   (incometax, EPFO) get two-factor the day you create the account.
3. **Annual audit of the list, one line each.** Portals move (the TRACES URL drifts, IRDAI's portal was
   renamed); a five-minute check each April keeps the folder from rotting.
4. **One page printed for the family.** The fraud-reporting portal, the ombudsman, DigiLocker, the e-filing
   login — on paper in the house file, because the moment you need the cyber-crime portal most is the moment
   your phone may be the compromised device.
5. **Verify a new portal once, then never search for it again.** The most dangerous moment in this system is
   the first search for a portal you have not bookmarked; every later visit should come from the folder.

## The bottom line

The official-portal layer is the part of Indian personal finance that is genuinely better than anywhere else
in the world: every rupee you have is checkable, correctable and claimable online, free, by its owner. The
cost of that system is the navigation problem — and the fraud industry that lives inside the navigation
problem. Thirty bookmarks, one synced folder, one printed page. That is the entire infrastructure, and it
does more for your financial security than any product you can buy this year.
